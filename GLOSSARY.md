# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses, watch lessons and track their progress.

## Language

### Money back

**Refund**:
A learner's money for a Purchase given back in full through Cadence, at the learner's request and with approval from a Support Team Member. It ends the Purchase and the learner's access to the course, but not their progress history. There are no partial Refunds, and no money back that leaves access in place.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, reversal, money back, partial refund

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask for a Refund on one of their Purchases. It is Requested until it is reviewed, then either Approved (which produces a Refund) or Declined (which changes nothing).
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), cancellation request, reversal request

**Refund Window**:
The period in which a learner may raise a Refund Request: from the day of the Purchase through the fourteenth calendar day after it, with that last day included in full.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window, cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may raise a Refund Request on a Purchase right now.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, cancellable

**Revocation**:
Ending a learner's access to a course without giving money back. Out of scope: Cadence does not support Revocations today.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, ban

**Chargeback**:
Money for a Purchase pulled back by the learner's bank or card issuer through a dispute, without Cadence's approval. Distinct from a Refund. Out of scope: Cadence does not handle Chargebacks today.
_Avoid_: Refund, dispute refund
